M.S. student at SNU

After receiving my B.S. from Yonsei University, I joined Seoul National University as an M.S. student in Artificial Intelligence, where I am advised by Prof. H. Jin Kim in the Lab for Autonomous Robotics Research (LARR).

My research interests include robotics, robot learning, agentic AI, and vision-language-action (VLA) models. Recently, I have been working on embodied coding agents that write, test, and refine programs.
